Our Story

Marble Crafted with Passion & Precision

Based in Karachi with quarrying operations across Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Tijarah Marble has been transforming raw stone into beautiful surfaces for over 25 years. Our factory on Manghopir Road combines traditional stone-cutting craftsmanship with modern CNC technology to deliver flawless finishes every time.

We supply architects, interior designers, builders, and homeowners across Pakistan and export to the Middle East, Europe, and beyond.
